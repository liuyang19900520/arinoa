Arinoa is a beauty camera that keeps you looking like yourself — just on a good day.

REAL-TIME BEAUTY
• Smooth skin, brighten, and refine with live preview before you shoot
• Face-aware effects powered by on-device Vision
• Presets for a quick natural look, with fine-tuning sliders

FILTERS & STYLES
• Film-inspired color styles you can preview live
• Works for photos and videos (with sound)

RETOUCH EXISTING PHOTOS
• Pick a photo from your library and apply the same beauty effects

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All processing happens on your iPhone
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no uploads
• Photos and videos are saved only to your own photo library

LANGUAGES
• English, 日本語 and 简体中文, switchable inside the app
